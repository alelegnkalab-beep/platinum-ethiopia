# Platinum Ethiopia

Build a mobile-first Amharic auction app named "ፕላቲኒየም ኢትዮጵያ" (Platinum Ethiopia) with a sleek dark platinum and blue design.

Include these exact rules and features:

1. Welcome Bonus & Currency: Users get 100 points (10 ETB) immediately upon joining. 100 points = 10 ETB.

2. Minimum Limit: Users need at least 1,000 points (100 ETB) to withdraw money or use points to place a bid.

3. Auctions: Every item starts at 50 ETB and runs for 21 days. Highest bidder after 21 days wins.

4. Product Feed (Vertical Scroll): Each product card must have a horizontal swipeable color/image slider, 21-day timer, views count, bidders count, "Details" popup, Like button, Comment section, and Share button.

5. AI Auto-Posting: Show a banner explaining AI automatically posts 5 new electronics products every day at 1:00 AM (7:00 LT) starting at 50 ETB. Pre-load 5 electronics items (iPhone 15 Pro Max, Samsung S24 Ultra, MacBook Pro M3, PS5 Slim, Sony WH-1000XM5).

6. Bottom Dashboard Bar: Home (መነሻ), Tasks (ታስክ - 5 social media follow tasks giving 100 points each), Daily Check-in (ዴይሊ - 20 points), Referral (ሪፈራል - 50 points), Wallet (ዋሌት - shows points & ETB balance + Withdraw button), and Airdrop (ኤርድሮፕ).

7. Top-Right 3-Dots Menu: Account & Profile, AI Data schedule, Features, FAQ, and Login/Logout.
